Web Binary Privacy Policy

Web Binary, registered in Ragama, Sri Lanka, is dedicated to maintaining the highest levels of privacy and data protection for our global user base. This policy is formulated in strict accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act No. 09 of 2022 (Sri Lanka) and the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). We act as a Data Controller for the personal information collected through our platform, ensuring that all processing activities are grounded in the principles of lawfulness, fairness, and transparency. We collect only the data necessary to provide our professional networking and cybersecurity services, which may include identity information, contact details, and professional credentials. Under no circumstances is sensitive data processed without explicit, informed consent, and all such data is subjected to enhanced security measures, including AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS/SSL protocols during transmission.

We empower our users with comprehensive data subject rights, allowing you to access, rectify, or request the erasure of your personal information at any time. Our Data Protection Officer (DPO) ensures that all requests are handled within 30 days, as mandated by international standards. Furthermore, as a global entity, Web Binary facilitates cross-border data transfers only when the receiving jurisdiction provides an adequate level of protection or through the implementation of Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Our records retention policy ensures that your data is only kept for as long as is strictly necessary for the fulfillment of our services or as required by Sri Lankan financial and legal statutes, after which it is destroyed using secure, unrecoverable methods.

Web Binary Cookie Policy

To provide a seamless and secure experience on the Web Binary platform, we utilize cookies and similar tracking technologies. These small data files are stored on your device to help us recognize you upon return, maintain your session security, and personalize your professional feed. Our cookies are categorized into three areas: Essential, Performance, and Preference. Essential cookies are mandatory for the operation of the site, facilitating secure logins and protecting against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF). Performance cookies allow us to analyze aggregate traffic patterns, helping our IT team optimize server load and speed for users connecting from different global regions. Preference cookies remember your settings, such as language choices and display themes, ensuring a consistent user experience.

Web Binary respects your right to choose. While most web browsers automatically accept cookies, you can modify your browser settings to decline them or alert you when a cookie is being sent. However, please be advised that disabling essential cookies may significantly impact the functionality of our platform, particularly for Premium members who require secure, authenticated access to restricted research and networking areas. We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal data for sale to third-party advertisers. Our use of tracking technology is strictly limited to improving platform performance and ensuring the security of our professional community, in alignment with the Personal Data Protection Act of Sri Lanka and the EU ePrivacy Directive.
